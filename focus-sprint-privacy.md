# Focus Sprint Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 24, 2026

Focus Sprint is a mobile productivity app designed to help users run focus timers, track focus progress, and build a daily focus habit.

## Information We Collect

Focus Sprint stores your timer progress, checklist choices, streaks, session history, settings, and reminder preferences locally on your device.

If analytics are enabled, we may collect basic app usage events such as timer starts, completed focus sessions, settings changes, ad events, and onboarding completion. These events help us understand which features are useful and improve the app.

If ads are enabled, Google AdMob may collect or process device, advertising, and usage data to show ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Focus Sprint does not require an account.

We do not intentionally collect your name, email address, contacts, calendar, photos, precise location, health data, or payment information in the current version of the app.

## Local Storage

Focus Sprint uses local device storage to save app state, including completed sessions, streak progress, checklist settings, timer preferences, and reminder settings.

Deleting the app may delete this local data.

## Analytics

Focus Sprint may use PostHog or a similar analytics provider to collect product analytics events. These events are used to understand app usage, diagnose issues, and improve the product.

Analytics events are designed to avoid personal content and are limited to app interaction data.

## Advertising

Focus Sprint may use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.

AdMob may use device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interaction data, diagnostics, and other data according to Google's policies.

Where required, Focus Sprint shows a consent flow before requesting personalized ads. Users may manage eligible ad privacy choices from the app settings.

## Notifications

Focus Sprint can send local reminders if you enable them. Notification permission is requested only when you turn on reminders.

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally through the mobile operating system.

## Sharing

Focus Sprint includes a share feature that opens the native share sheet with a summary of your focus progress. You choose whether and where to share that content.

## Children

Focus Sprint is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Third-Party Services

Focus Sprint may use:

- Google AdMob for ads and ad measurement.
- PostHog for product analytics.
- Expo and React Native services for app functionality.

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Analytics and advertising data may be retained by third-party providers according to their policies.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Disable reminders from inside the app.
- Manage eligible ad privacy choices from app settings.
- Limit ad tracking from your device settings.
- Delete the app to remove locally stored app data.
