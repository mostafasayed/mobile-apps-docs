# HydroFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026

HydroFlow is a hydration tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information We Store

HydroFlow stores the following information locally on your device:

- Daily water intake
- Daily goal
- Cup size
- Streak and best streak
- Weekly hydration history
- Reminder settings
- Selected theme
- Unlocked themes and streak freeze rewards

This information is stored on your device using local app storage. HydroFlow does not require an account and does not send this hydration data to our own server.

## Notifications

HydroFlow can schedule local hydration reminders if you enable reminders. These reminders are created on your device. HydroFlow does not send push notification tokens to our own server.

## Advertising

HydroFlow uses Google AdMob to show ads, including banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics, and ad performance data. This information is handled by Google according to Google's policies.

HydroFlow uses Google consent tools where required to request advertising consent and to support non-personalized ads when appropriate.

You can learn more about how Google uses data here:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Rewarded Ads

HydroFlow may offer optional rewarded ads. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, you may receive in-app rewards such as premium themes or streak freezes. Rewarded ads are optional.

## App Store Reviews

HydroFlow may ask whether you want to rate or review the app after you have used it successfully. Review prompts are handled by the app store platform.

## Data Sharing

HydroFlow does not sell your personal information. Advertising data may be processed by Google AdMob as described above.

## Children

HydroFlow is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through advertising or platform services, contact the relevant platform provider.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Disable notifications in your device settings.
- Reset or delete app data by clearing HydroFlow storage or uninstalling the app.
- Manage ad personalization in your device or Google account settings.
- Use app store privacy controls where available.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at the same public URL.
